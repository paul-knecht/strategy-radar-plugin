Example Pharma, internal planning note: filling capacity for prefilled syringes (August 2026)

Purpose. This note sets out how the manufacturing planning team sees the supply of filled prefilled syringes over the next three years. It is for internal use only and is not to be shared outside Example Pharma.

Current position. Our products are filled partly at our own plant and partly by outside providers under annual contracts. The new line at Site Y will add our own capacity, but it will not be qualified before 2027.

Outlook. Our outside filling contracts will cover less of our syringe volume in 2027 than they do today. Two providers have told us that they cannot extend our slots, and a third has offered slots only at prices we have not yet agreed to.

Options under review. We are comparing three routes: more own lines, longer outside contracts, and buying a working plant. The planning team has not yet costed the third route.

Next steps. Manufacturing planning will bring a costed comparison of the three routes to the management board in the autumn. Until then, no new outside contracts should be signed for periods longer than one year.
