Example Pharma starts building its own filling line in Country Y

Town Y, 2 July 2026. Example Pharma today confirmed that work has begun on a new sterile filling line at its Site Y plant in Country Y.

Construction of the new line began in March 2026 and the line is scheduled to start filling in 2027. The line will be used only for Example Pharma's own products.

The new line is a single high-speed unit for prefilled syringes. The company said demand for its prefilled syringes has outgrown the filling capacity it can book from outside providers. "We looked at buying more outside capacity first," said the head of manufacturing at Example Pharma. "The slots we need are not available on terms we can plan around, so we are building."

Once the line reaches full speed it is expected to fill up to 40 million syringes a year. Example Pharma will keep using outside providers for some older products while it qualifies the line with the regulators in Country Y.

The company said the investment does not change its plans for research and development, which continue at the Town Y campus. Example Pharma added that it will review the balance between its own lines and outside providers every year.

About Example Pharma
Example Pharma develops and markets injectable medicines and is headquartered in Town Y. This announcement contains forward-looking statements. Actual results may differ because of regulatory timing and construction delays.
