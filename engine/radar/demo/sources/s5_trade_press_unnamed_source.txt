Competitor C may open part of its new plant to outside customers, source says

By a staff reporter. 3 September 2026.

Competitor C is considering letting outside drug makers use part of the filling plant it bought last year in Country Y, according to a person familiar with the matter who asked not to be named.

Competitor C could offer one of the plant's two filling lines to outside customers once its own products have been moved over, the person said. The person described the talks about such a filling partnership as early and said no customer had signed.

Competitor C declined to comment on the report. A spokesperson referred earlier questions to the statement issued when Competitor C completed its purchase of the filling plant in Country Y with its two filling lines, in which the company said the plant would be used for its own products.

Industry analysts contacted by this publication said open filling capacity remains scarce. One contract provider has told its customers it is fully booked for more than a year. "Anyone who offers a line to outsiders will find takers quickly," said an analyst at a small consultancy, who is not named here because the firm does not allow it.

Separately, Competitor D is said to be weighing a new filling site in Country Z, although the source gave no size or date. Competitor D did not respond to a request for comment.

The report could not be independently confirmed. This publication has not seen any document that supports it, and the person who provided the information may have an interest in how the market reads it.
