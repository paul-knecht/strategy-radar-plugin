Competitor B, annual filing for 2025 (extract): Properties and capacity

Item 2. Properties and capacity

Competitor B provides contract filling of injectable medicines for other drug makers. It does not sell medicines under its own name.

Our site in Country Z began commercial filling in January 2026 and now runs three filling lines. All three lines are open to third-party customers under multi-year contracts. The three lines have a combined capacity of 45 million vials a year.

Contract filling slots at the Country Z site are fully booked through the end of 2027. We have received requests from customers for more slots than we can offer, and we have not accepted any request for capacity that we cannot deliver on time.

We have no current plans to add lines beyond the three at the Country Z site. Any decision to add lines would depend on customer commitments and on the time needed to qualify new equipment.

Risk factors

Our business depends on a small number of large customers. If customers decide to fill their products in their own plants instead of using ours, demand for our services could fall. New filling capacity built by other companies could also lower the prices we can charge. Delays in qualifying equipment or in receiving regulatory approvals could postpone the start of production for our customers.
