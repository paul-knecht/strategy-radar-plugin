Competitor A adds filling lines to keep supply in-house

16 May 2026. Competitor A said this week that it will add two filling lines at Plant North in Country X, a move that keeps the new capacity for its own products.

According to the company's announcement, Competitor A will add two filling lines at Plant North, the lines will fill only Competitor A's own injectable products, and production is planned to start in 2028. The company is also building a smaller plant, Plant South in Country Y.

The announcement comes as other drug makers weigh whether to build or buy filling capacity. Competitor D, which has not announced new lines, declined to say whether it would follow.

Competitor A's chief operating officer said that controlling filling capacity protects supply and launch plans. The company does not plan to offer the new lines to other drug makers, according to the announcement.

New filling lines take time to build and qualify, so the effect on the market will not be felt for some years. Until then, drug makers that rely on outside providers will keep competing for the slots that exist today.

This article is based on the company's announcement. The publication did not carry out its own checks of the plans described.
