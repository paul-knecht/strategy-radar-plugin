Competitor A to expand its own filling capacity in Country X and Country Y

Capital City, 14 May 2026. Competitor A today announced an investment in sterile manufacturing to support the growth of its injectable medicines.

Competitor A will add two filling lines at Plant North in Country X. The two new lines will fill only Competitor A's own injectable products, and production is planned to start in 2028. Construction contracts will be awarded in the coming months.

The company is also building a smaller facility. Plant South in Country Y, now under construction, will add one filling line reserved for Competitor A's own products and is expected to start operating in 2027.

"Our injectable range has grown faster than our ability to fill it," said the chief operating officer of Competitor A. "Controlling our own filling capacity lets us protect supply for patients and keep our launch plans on schedule."

Competitor A does not plan to offer the new lines to other drug makers. The company said it will keep buying filling services from outside providers for a small number of older products until the new lines are running.

About Competitor A
Competitor A develops and manufactures injectable medicines and is headquartered in Capital City. This announcement contains forward-looking statements. Actual results may differ because of regulatory timing, construction delays and changes in demand.
